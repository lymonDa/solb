# SOLB — Google Search Ads Campaign Blueprint

**Brand:** صلب | SOLB  
**Market:** Riyadh, Saudi Arabia  
**Objective:** Drive qualified phone inquiries and WhatsApp conversations for concrete cutting and coring services in Riyadh.  
**Ad Platform:** Google Search Ads (Paid Search)  
**Status:** Recommended Campaign Architecture (Pending Account Launch & Verification)  

---

## 1. Executive Strategy & Commercial Principles

1. **Service-Dedicated Landing Pages**: Never route paid search traffic for specific services (e.g., "تخريم كور") to the homepage. Each ad group directs users to a specialized landing page that mirrors the exact query intent.
2. **Mobile-First Intent**: In the Riyadh construction sector, >80% of contractor searches occur on mobile devices. Every landing page features immediate 1-tap phone and WhatsApp CTAs within the first viewport and a sticky bottom contact bar.
3. **Budget Protection via Rigorous Negatives**: High click-waste occurs on queries seeking equipment purchases, tool rentals without operators, DIY advice, and non-Riyadh locations. Extensive negative lists prevent irrelevant ad spend.
4. **Honest Conversion Definition**: A click on `tel:+966556123725` or `https://wa.me/966556123725` is an **intent-to-contact proxy conversion**, not a guaranteed completed job or contract.

## 7. Bidding Strategy & Measurement Evolution

1. **Phase 1 (Launch & Discovery — Weeks 1 to 4)**:
   - **Bidding Strategy**: **Maximize Clicks** with a Maximum CPC bid limit (to prevent bidding spikes during early learning).
   - **Goal**: Gather high-intent search query data, validate negative keyword lists, and establish baseline CTR and CTA click rates.
2. **Phase 2 (Conversion Optimization — Week 5+)**:
   - Once the account records 30+ verified `phone_click` and `whatsapp_click` proxy events within 30 days:
   - Switch to **Maximize Conversions** (or Target CPA based on historical conversion cost).
   - Continue monitoring search terms weekly to add newly discovered negative terms.
